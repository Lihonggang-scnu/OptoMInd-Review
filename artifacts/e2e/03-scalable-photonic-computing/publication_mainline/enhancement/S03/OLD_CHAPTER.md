Photonic matrix multiplication engines exploit the natural superposition and interference of light to perform parallel dot-product operations at the speed of light with energy consumption primarily determined by optical power rather than charge movement [REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51]. In these engines, the output field amplitude is proportional to the dot product of input vector and weight matrix elements, enabling massive parallelism within a single computational step [REF:d077ba1e200eb573e493e87abf08c66338d1819d][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. This physical mechanism provides a theoretical advantage in energy per multiply-accumulate operation for high-throughput inference tasks, since the energy scales with transmitted optical power instead of the resistive heating associated with electronic MAC units [REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. The integrated photonic tensor cores further extend this principle by performing parallel convolutional processing through the same interference-based dot-product mechanism, maintaining energy efficiency proportional to optical power throughput [REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd].

Mainstream implementations of optical matrix-vector multiplication for photonic tensor cores utilize three principal hardware platforms: free-space diffraction systems, integrated Mach-Zehnder interferometer arrays, and micro-ring resonator networks [REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. These platforms rest on photonic crystals—periodically structured dielectric composites that manipulate electromagnetic wave propagation and form the basis for various nanophotonic components deployed in computing applications [REF:ba148932cfce8cfe4b50a0a1339c616af940900c][REF:15ccb57280963ed7debeebb95d5a8f2418fb55d3][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. Each architecture presents distinct trade-offs: free-space systems offer large aperture and inherent parallelism but are bulky and difficult to integrate on-chip, while integrated MZI and MRR solutions provide compact footprints at the cost of requiring extremely precise phase control, complex feedback circuits, and facing scalability limitations imposed by waveguide crosstalk and propagation loss.

The design of photonic matrix multiplication engines has evolved from intuitive systematic fine-tuning to sophisticated inverse-design methodologies leveraging deep learning and topology optimization [REF:ae237e70ca4fd23980ced32406ed67668edf25c8][REF:15ccb57280963ed7debeebb95d5a8f2418fb55d3][REF:identity-fallback:e49491a3a2a5b595][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. Topology optimization and level-set formulations serve as primary computational approaches for designing these engines, enabling the inverse design of complex nanophotonic structures to achieve specific linear transformation functions [REF:d077ba1e200eb573e493e87abf08c66338d1819d][REF:identity-fallback:d010fda904f7c745]. Density-based topology optimization treats permittivity of every pixel as an independent design variable, allowing continuous variation during optimization before applying filtering and projection techniques to ensure manufacturable binary distributions compatible with lithographic fabrication processes. Level-set methods represent geometry implicitly through a higher-dimensional scalar function, evolving material boundaries via velocity fields that naturally encourage structural integrity. Physics-inspired neural operators and deep learning methods are being explored to accelerate simulation and design of photonic computing components, offering potentially faster alternatives to traditional finite element methods and reducing the computational overhead that previously constrained design iteration [REF:5e85872741adcc3796a092c6c772ec882a8248e0][REF:066d90a11578707661a773bfc68cc05bd6b3e435][REF:identity-fallback:8b60e3709a8988ac][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. Inverse design methodologies including adjoint solvers and physics-informed neural networks enable the optimization of critical engine components such as polarization beam splitters and waveguide couplers to meet strict foundry fabrication constraints [REF:8ab7d5638952e89b5ac01f349668839fd258d693][REF:d45611ec6c8ef616676ecbb1851bf6a398a23967][REF:72104901d05262d178fce1a028164d85f5fba671][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. Heuristic optimization algorithms are generally preferred over non-heuristic direct search methods in photonic inverse design to avoid convergence to local minima in high-dimensional parameter spaces [REF:cea9e6112f363e19aa754d2681622ab07650c71c][REF:cf3bd470c716ddca24f0a26b85b4bdccd5bc2797][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. Furthermore, physics-aware neural networks and machine learning surrogates are employed to predict effective refractive indices and optimize photonic device designs, directly addressing fabrication limitations and complexity in the development of scalable photonic computing engines [REF:469e99601eb8d7d7b5493684cfae88717bd305f0][REF:fcc67514a196a07bd42ee9825edbf14be0854847][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a].

Inverse-designed nanophotonic neural network accelerators have been demonstrated for image classification tasks using benchmark datasets like MNIST, showing promising scalability with increasing network complexity [REF:c0a2dd7d6fabe134e59ca64fa09cfbb3552f90cb][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. These accelerators utilize inverse-designed nanophotonic circuits to encode compressed input features onto coherent optical fields, enabling ultra-compact matrix multiplication operations that bypass traditional electronic interconnect bottlenecks [REF:c0a2dd7d6fabe134e59ca64fa09cfbb3552f90cb][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. Such demonstrations validate the feasibility of photonic computing engines as practical acceleration substrates for specific workload classes.

Analog photonic computation faces fundamental precision constraints arising from multiple physical mechanisms. The fundamental precision of analog photonic computation is constrained by the quantization imposed by photodetection and thermal noise, limiting the effective bit-resolution compared to digital electronic counterparts [REF:doi:10.3390/nano14080697][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. Analog photonic computation is subject to additional precision limitations imposed by detector noise, thermal drift, and finite extinction ratios, which restrict numerical accuracy relative to digital electronic MAC units and constrain algorithmic applicability to low-precision inference workloads [REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. Fabrication-induced phase variations introduce undesired optical phase noises in coherent Mach-Zehnder interferometer-based nanophotonic neural networks, leading to faulty matrix-vector multiplication and further limiting the numerical precision achievable in practice [REF:955db7b00b8b503d46c2749d399075e11f40c61b][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. Thermal crosstalk in integrated thermo-optic attenuators and phase shifters can induce undesired coupling between adjacent computational elements, imposing a boundary condition on the density and performance of programmable photonic circuits [REF:35b0156c1b02ea4d54610e852cecd49b7b505a71][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. Resonator-based photonic computing elements are additionally vulnerable to environmental perturbations, specifically temperature fluctuations and mechanical vibrations, which introduce phase noise that degrades computational precision [REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7]. Collectively, these noise sources establish a hard ceiling on the numerical fidelity attainable by analog photonic engines without active calibration and error-correction overhead.

The absence of native nonlinearity in passive photonic circuits imposes a fundamental constraint on deep network training, requiring external or hybrid nonlinear insertion points that complicate scalable integration [REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. Deep learning models introduce nonlinearity through activation functions and backpropagation, contrasting sharply with the inherent linearity of passive photonic components, necessitating external or hybrid mechanisms for nonlinear operations in photonic computing engines [REF:60df2f3598ea7d56ae24a649121907a6769eb444]. Physical neural networks based on photonic systems typically assume linearity and dissipation-free evolution, which excludes many realistic physical systems characterized by nonlinearity and loss, limiting the direct applicability of backpropagation-trained models without hybrid nonlinear insertion [REF:8708abfc6a5d80c1d2bbd911677e2f8940040a4c]. The absence of native nonlinearity necessitates external or hybrid nonlinear insertion points for deep learning applications, introducing latency and energy overhead that constrains the performance envelope relative to electronic counterparts [REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. Implementing activation functions in photonic computing requires either electro-optic conversion followed by electronic nonlinear processing or the integration of nonlinear optical materials whose response characteristics must be carefully engineered [REF:doi:10.3390/nano14080697][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a].

Taken together, these physical and architectural factors define an operating envelope where analog photonic computation excels in high-throughput, low-precision inference tasks where the precision limitations imposed by photodetection quantization and thermal drift are algorithmically tolerable [REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd][REF:4e103f1639b938e4c48c8da29d305caae373fc55][REF:ed14198e3579a78f1802b519db669f0ec2185d51][REF:f7bd07322c2336c8e12a77f9c4303d2a8768892a]. Analog photonic matrix multiplication engines face fundamental precision limits due to the absence of native nonlinearity in linear optical circuits and the quantization noise introduced by photodetection, which constrains their applicability to high-precision training tasks [REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd]. Photonic matrix multiplication engines offer energy-efficient parallel computation for high-throughput, low-precision inference scenarios, though they lack the numerical precision required for gradient-based training without hybrid assistance [REF:0c5bb1f1c962af9e250c63c2d759da643968a1cd]. This positioning establishes photonic tensor cores not as general-purpose replacements for electronic processors, but as specialized co-processors targeting inference workloads where energy efficiency and throughput outweigh the need for full numerical precision.
