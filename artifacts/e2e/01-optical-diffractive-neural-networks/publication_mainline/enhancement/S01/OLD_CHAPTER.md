Diffractive neural layers implement linear transformations via wave propagation governed by Fourier optics, where the optical field evolves through free space and phase-modulating layers according to diffraction integrals rather than discrete matrix multiplication [REF:f7b7631268f17b1f868015df0bd08ef57904024d][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. Under scalar diffraction theory, the complex field at each layer is computed as the Fourier transform of the previous layer's field modulated by a phase profile and propagated over an axial distance [REF:bb2489d876aa7ecf8853de5b1b0a96e4d303d1cd][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. This physics-first formulation replaces the abstract weight matrices of digital neural networks with physically realized spatially varying phase structures, making the computational expressivity of diffractive networks fundamentally bounded by the laws of wave optics rather than by algorithmic design choices alone. The resulting architecture naturally supports ultra-high bandwidth and highly parallel computation because every point on a diffractive layer interacts simultaneously with the entire incoming optical field.

In two-dimensional diffractive optical neural networks with a hard circular pupil, the point spread function is described by a Bessel function of the first kind, and its Fourier transform is flat over the pupil aperture, establishing the fundamental spatial frequency response of single-plane diffractive layers [REF:7caf7c29ac69aa12038f926409fb469f65ebeb2a][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. Diffractive deep neural networks implement linear transformations through cascaded phase-only modulation planes, where the optical field propagates via diffraction between layers to approximate matrix-vector multiplication without electronic weights [REF:e328fde07bd3248d2019054a4cdbf0f61e519369][REF:5a168527cffb2bbaa672547b16bdf8768fc179b7][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. This cascade structure transforms what would be a sequence of discrete matrix operations into a continuous wave-optics problem, where each inter-plane propagation step encodes a portion of the overall linear transformation through the accumulated phase shifts imparted by the intervening diffractive surfaces.

Single-plane diffractive optical elements enable compact, single-element imaging systems that overcome the space and complexity requirements of traditional multi-lens optical systems [REF:a9abfc26735562bc526ac130ae07afd070e3151b][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. Diffractive deep neural networks perform arbitrary functions by utilizing diffraction across cascaded diffractive surfaces, distinguishing them from single-layer diffractive elements that can only implement limited mappings [REF:de17df4151a9cd12e1db11fe8e684f88150ead7b][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. The transition from single-plane to multi-plane architectures represents a fundamental shift in computational capacity: while a single DOE can approximate a fixed linear operator, multiple trainable planes collectively learn an increasingly rich family of transformations. Multi-layer diffractive architectures provide significantly better performance for generalization and application-specific design compared to single-plane designs, but introduce practical challenges related to fabrication complexity and opto-mechanical alignment sensitivity [REF:doi:10.1515/nanoph-2020-0291][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. These alignment tolerances scale unfavorably with layer count, creating a practical ceiling on deployable depth that does not exist in purely electronic networks.

The differentiability of diffractive propagation with respect to physical parameters such as meta-atom dimensions and inter-plane spacing enables gradient-based training of optical weights directly from input-output data pairs [REF:52cf7f6ca6ab9c9c241b36bca83c962551dd3480][REF:88f87b34acdb11cf20ad16d6fbdd5399e8617f8f][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. While early designs utilized fixed or programmable metasurfaces for specific tasks, modern trainable architectures employ multi-plane configurations where both phase and amplitude modulation are optimized during training to enhance computational expressivity [REF:7efb4f8ff9aa6f5e9691e9392b539298ef46ca04][REF:52cf7f6ca6ab9c9c241b36bca83c962551dd3480][REF:7991c4cd79e5e08f9ca67e6a5fd5b509b9c2f4ac][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. This co-design paradigm—where the physical structure and learning algorithm are jointly optimized—distinguishes diffractive neural networks from earlier optical computing proposals that relied on hand-engineered optical components. The training process back-propagates loss gradients through the analytically differentiable forward model of wave propagation, allowing the system to discover non-intuitive phase patterns that maximize task performance.

The Abbe-Rayleigh diffraction limit imposes a fundamental physical constraint on the spatial resolution of diffractive neural networks, requiring near-field techniques or superoscillatory designs to resolve micro- and nano-scale features that exceed traditional optical equipment capabilities [REF:5c084d119ead6e728fa85d3ea4d10ab49295829b][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. The physical architecture of diffractive layers relies on multilayer thin-film structures where layer thicknesses and material properties are optimized to control phase shifts, serving as the hardware substrate for trainable optical weights in inverse-designed photonic platforms [REF:42e53c9c7630eb735f9a67f329c948423910cd8d][REF:c977f316916c59a12a847854a4eef6f49381b590]. These structural color coatings and functional thin-film stacks provide the nanoscale precision necessary to encode learned phase profiles, though fabrication tolerances at the sub-wavelength scale remain a persistent engineering challenge.

Diffractive deep neural networks (D2NNs) implement all-optical inference by replacing electronic weight matrices with spatially varying phase profiles that modulate light via Fresnel diffraction, enabling real-time, energy-efficient classification without active power consumption during the forward pass [REF:56f68ce02f6656afc6453655e57a5ceb11c970e0][REF:9f31d98e42fb579c04a4b4255b7bc65fd68bd49d]. Diffractive Deep Neural Networks (D2NNs) implement optical machine learning by using computer-designed diffraction patterns to perform forward propagation, replacing electronic weight matrices with spatially varying phase profiles that modulate light via Fresnel or Fraunhofer diffraction [REF:6664d1be229dafa7a65e2b7fb1243f29084bb0a4][REF:94f3822c453a2ab6d44c59f1f60026acb15739af][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. Diffractive Deep Neural Networks replace electronic weight matrices with spatially varying phase profiles that implement linear transformations via wave propagation, addressing the energy and scaling limitations of Moore's Law in electronic computing [REF:0518ca584481f922f473ca4f510a272c0470b0cb][REF:e53516335e5d20873e80959eb99fd5f967d40b47][REF:1d81b4001ac0bcfeddb1ab3522f209251645373f][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. In these fully passive systems, information flows through the network at the speed of light with zero dynamic power dissipation during inference—the only energy cost being the illumination source and subsequent electronic readout at the detector plane.

Polarization multiplexing in a single diffractive network enables the all-optical implementation of a group of complex-valued arbitrary linear transformations simultaneously at the same output field of view [REF:04c16f79232a1dba281da8a2626e1933bcb10641]. Multi-task learning in diffractive networks is achieved by co-designing hardware structures and software algorithms, allowing a single physical architecture to perform diverse classification or processing tasks without reconfiguration [REF:7991c4cd79e5e08f9ca67e6a5fd5b509b9c2f4ac][REF:88f87b34acdb11cf20ad16d6fbdd5399e8617f8f][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. By encoding different computational tasks into distinct polarization states, spatial regions, or wavelength channels, a single fabricated diffractive element can serve as a multi-functional processor, dramatically reducing the hardware footprint required for heterogeneous computing workloads.

Optical computing paradigms leveraging light propagation offer inherent advantages in parallel processing, high throughput, and energy efficiency compared to conventional electronic neural network implementations [REF:9519307d904c6434161063811ff8e3bff4692d01][REF:18736e817b036394ff6925eefbc4305014dcde77][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. The primary architectural advantage of diffractive neural networks lies in their ultra-low latency, low power consumption, and highly parallel optical computing capability compared to traditional electronic implementations [REF:13eb4b9451efe574ec7d18cbd4e81218b57bbbd8][REF:b539a6464c7a7878f297b022317270b770af22eb][REF:4ec17510a4fcc9d4756694cc4efa9bba74b83503][REF:89798a664bae396ed10c9e343a595b99dd96451b][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. Optical neural networks offer ultra-high bandwidth, high calculation speed, and high parallelism compared to conventional electronic computing devices due to the inherent properties of light propagation [REF:585c02f3ea677e72535f4ea2af006230e5892445][REF:c3878ee93c5882c47cf566762644e36fe997e521][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. These advantages stem from the fundamental nature of wave propagation: every pixel on every diffractive layer performs a weighted sum operation simultaneously, with no clock cycle or sequential memory access bottleneck.

All-optical diffractive deep neural networks often suffer from complex optical setups, motivating hybrid approaches that simplify the physical architecture [REF:adb51917e624e16d5f8f4c4b793878218e326bf2][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. Multi-layer diffractive optical neural networks can perform machine learning tasks at the speed of light with low energy consumption by leveraging passive photon propagation [REF:f295236e2bd30dd07a40fd3ac8a44d9f6af6a373][REF:a7167251b6fb2cfa3339ae6a9eb0367e69dd78f0][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. Diffractive deep neural networks support multifunctional processing capabilities, including multi-task learning and multi-directional beam steering, enabled by learned structured phase profiles [REF:4ec17510a4fcc9d4756694cc4efa9bba74b83503][REF:309e04eb403dcdb92defdd17d845540f678a41bb][REF:cf6a6dfb9965507bd6c90708db3104eb8e5b8750][REF:5c7e5248d9eb7f373f10277410bf8506160907ea][REF:77474cdcf483b25f73dacf20deb295cd1c2945b7][REF:58f461486dd3c713b61d5ed0d374aa0d680dc31c]. Hybrid optical-electronic architectures combine the computational throughput of passive diffractive front-ends with the flexibility of electronic post-processing, achieving a practical balance between all-optical speed and programmable adaptability that pure all-optical systems struggle to match.
