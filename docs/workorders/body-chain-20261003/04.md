# WO-04 — Preserve current identities, usable material, and late-paper routing

## Gate

WAITING_APPROVAL. Begin only after explicit approval following WO-02 and WO-03.

## Scope

- `optomind_research/runtime/upgrade3/planning_material_triage.py`: current identity mapping and material admission.
- `optomind_research/runtime/upgrade3/progressive_review_plan.py`: material assembly, candidate adoption, source identity map, and formal packet construction.
- `optomind_research/runtime/upgrade3/chapter_arrangement.py`: source catalog and chapter-tool material handoff.
- `optomind_research/runtime/upgrade3/review_unit_writer.py`: writer source catalog/allowed-handle input.

## Actions

1. Map local-index material to the current `source_identity_map` before triage. Preserve paper ID, DOI, title, and other available identity; an old P-handle cannot stand in for a current paper merely because its string matches.
2. Admit material from outside the current pool through the existing identity and routing path. If identity cannot be resolved, keep the usable material and unresolved identity visible; do not borrow another paper's handle.
3. Check all real material channels, including `source_materials`, `candidate_materials`, directed reading, supplement, chapter-tool material, and nested `paragraph_briefs`. Distinguish identity-only from substantive content.
4. Permit a review/tool summary with a valid cited identity to support the original study's admission. A bare identity row with no study content stays a candidate and cannot generate invented findings.
5. When an adopted candidate is accepted, carry its actual material/summary relationship into the formal packet and writer input. Handle identity conflicts explicitly; do not splice an old identity to a new paper's material.
6. Add resolvable tool-only sources to `source_catalog` and writer allowed handles while keeping original-study content provenance in the tool material.
7. Add an incremental route for late papers using their known chapter/use context where available; otherwise call the existing router for that increment only. Do not require every late paper to become a case, but ensure it has a selection opportunity.

## Quality examples

An old handle and current handle pointing to different papers remain distinct; a tool-only source is citable; identity plus review-derived content passes admission; identity-only content does not become a fabricated case; an adopted candidate's material reaches the writer; a late paper enters only relevant chapter inputs.

## Dependencies and record

Use F05, F08, F09, and F11 evidence from `references/` and `advisor/body_design_continuity_20261003/`. Record identity maps, material channel, admission classification, source catalog entries, late-route destination, and unresolved conflicts. Keep local-only historical packet paths marked `LOCAL_ONLY`.

## Limits

No paid retrieval, full run, credentials, broad reindex, or new identity system. Do not hard-code the reviewed scientific topic or sample answers into prompts.
