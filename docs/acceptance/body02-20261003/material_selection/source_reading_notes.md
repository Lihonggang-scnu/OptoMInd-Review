# P0004 source-reading notes: PDAC TME and ICI

Source read: `F:\OptoMind-Review-2\outputs\paper_cards\20260922_microbiome_batch\materials\00d3d83d6571a7d9c15adbb84e0c371ce46d15a4\snapshot-5be0c4b8670f4d78bd64a766\READING_VIEW.md`.

These are source facts and conditions for root cross-check. They preserve the review's evidence level; no additional causal claim is inferred.

1. **PDAC heterogeneity includes the TME.** The review says PDAC phenotypes differ in stromal and immune-cell composition within the TME. **Location:** `block-2e97a6eff2bd2a9126ca`, Introduction.

2. **Gut dysbiosis is reported in human PDAC.** The review describes dysbiosis as a phenotypic feature of human PDAC and says microbes may affect carcinogenesis through inflammation and therapy response directly or via metabolites. **Location:** `block-b2921dc0c65b614ed532`, Introduction. Short source phrase: “gut dysbiosis has also emerged as a notable phenotypic feature of human PDAC patients.”

3. **Pancreatic colonization is an explicit premise.** The review says bacteria can be detected in the TME across cancers and that studies establish pancreatic bacterial colonization even under steady-state conditions. **Location:** `block-98a3b10505437db74073`, “Composition of the intratumoral microbiota.”

4. **Pushalkar used two PDAC models.** The cited study used spontaneous Ptf1a^Cre LSL-Kras^G12D (KC) mice and an orthotopic model in which KPC-derived tumour cells were injected into wild-type pancreas. Germ-free KC mice had delayed oncogenesis; orthotopic tumour growth fell after microbial depletion. **Location:** `block-44ce2126a77858a248ad`, “Microbiota promotes PDAC development.”

5. **The depletion cocktail is specified.** The orthotopic experiment used oral vancomycin, neomycin, metronidazole, amphotericin, ampicillin, and amphotericin B. **Location:** `block-44ce2126a77858a248ad`.

6. **TME changes after depletion are enumerated.** Protection was accompanied by more Th1 CD4+ and cytotoxic CD8+ tumour infiltration; TAM expression of MHC II, CD86, TNFα, IL-12, and IL-6 increased; MDSCs decreased. **Location:** `block-44ce2126a77858a248ad`.

7. **The review reports a T-cell-dependence test.** CD4+/CD8+ depletion and PD1-antibody reactivation experiments were interpreted as indicating that the benefit of microbiota depletion depends on induced T-cell-mediated responses. **Location:** `block-44ce2126a77858a248ad`.

8. **A second antibiotic model used a different cocktail.** In Kras^G12D PTEN^lox/+ mice, Thomas et al. administered streptomycin, gentamicin, bacitracin, and ciprofloxacin in drinking water; treated mice had fewer malignant lobules at 3 months. **Location:** `block-cbeb75a4092a3ab928bf`, same PDAC-development section.

9. **PDAC/anti-PD-1 evidence is preclinical and conditional.** In the “Influence of the microbiota on immunotherapy” section, the review says orthotopic PDAC tumours normally did not respond to anti-PD-1 but became sensitive after oral-antibiotic depletion, with increased CD4+/CD8+ activation in the TME. **Location:** `block-be6e1d6ee0c7a33c4f14`, “Influence of the microbiota on immunotherapy.”

10. **A human-to-mouse transplant result is reported with a limitation.** Transplanting microbiota from long-term-survival PDAC patients into mice was associated with more CD8+ activation and fewer CD4+ Foxp3+ cells; the review immediately says more studies are needed before microbiota signatures can predict future human immunotherapy response. **Location:** `block-be6e1d6ee0c7a33c4f14`.

11. **Remote TME effects are also described.** Lower tumour burden and increased immune-cell infiltration occurred after microbial depletion in subcutaneous PDAC xenografts lacking intratumoral bacteria, which the review presents as support for indirect immune or metabolite routes. **Location:** `block-fb785ef10dabe3ba8115`, “Microbiota-derived metabolites may affect PDAC development and treatment.”
