1: """Work order 04: unified figures, citations and cross-references.
2: 
3: Stage order (fixed by Astra review of the first implementation):
4: 
5: 1. Figure assets are attached to the HANDLE draft first — captions may carry
6:    ``[Pxxxx]`` citations, so they must exist before citation numbering.
7: 2. ONE function — :func:`build_delivery_citation_map` — then scans the
8:    figure-bearing handle draft in reading order (introduction, body prose,
9:    table rows, figure captions) and produces the final citation map.
10: 3. The reader draft is rendered from that map, and its old handle-form
11:    reference section is REPLACED by the numbered list generated from the
12:    same map.
13: 
14: Identity rules: citation tokens may be namespace-qualified — ``[ns::Pxxxx]``
15: resolves through the catalog whose ``namespace`` is ``ns``, while a bare
16: ``[Pxxxx]`` resolves through the PRIMARY catalog (the first one supplied).
17: Two runs' ``P0001`` are therefore different papers whenever the text carries
18: the disambiguated token; catalog order alone never decides ownership.
19: Unknown tokens stay visible in the text and in the report — nothing is
20: deleted or invented.
21: 
22: Figures attach by stable local ID (``FIG:<id>``); display numbers are
23: assigned by POSITION IN THE FINAL TEXT (an asset passed earlier but anchored
24: later is figure 2, not figure 1).  Asset files are copied into the stage's
25: ``assets/`` directory and referenced by a relative path that resolves from
26: the manuscript.  Markdown structure (table pipes, math, escapes) is
27: preserved: numbering rewrites only bracket citation tokens.
28: 
29: No model calls, no network: metadata comes from the local catalogs only, and
30: missing fields stay missing.
31: """
