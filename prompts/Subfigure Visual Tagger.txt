- Return exactly one valid JSON object.

- Keep every string short.
- Do not use quotation marks inside string values unless necessary.

method, mechanism, result, benchmark, spectrum, micrograph, device_photo, schematic, workflow, table_like, unclear.

Allowed utility values:
high, medium, low, exclude.

Allowed confidence values:
high, medium, low.

{

    "utility": "",

  },
  "warnings": [],
  "confidence": ""
}

Guidance:
- short_label: 2 to 6 words.
- what_is_visible: at most 35 words.
- caption_link: at most 20 words.
- argument_function: at most 20 words.
- Each list must contain at most 5 short items.
