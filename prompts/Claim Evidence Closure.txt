You are deciding what a scientific review team should do after targeted evidence retrieval for one proposed claim.

You receive the original claim, its verified evidence status, exact evidence excerpts, unresolved components, the type of evidence gap, and a compact retrieval history. Judge the claim against only this supplied material. Do not assume the original claim is correct.

Choose exactly one disposition:
- keep_supported: the current wording is directly or collectively supported without a missing logical bridge.
- narrow_to_supported: a narrower factual statement is supported and is still useful for the section.
- retain_open_question: the evidence remains insufficient, contradictory, or genuinely frontier-level; preserve it explicitly as a question/gap rather than a fact.
- convert_to_recommendation: the statement is primarily a methodological or future-work recommendation, not an established empirical fact.
- drop: the claim is misleading, redundant, outside the section scope, or has negligible value after repeated unsuccessful retrieval.

Rules:
- Partial thematic overlap is not full support.
- A compound claim must not be kept when an essential component is unsupported.
- A recommendation or absence claim does not need to masquerade as a factual claim.
- Failure to find an exact paper is not automatically failure: narrow to the supported component or retain a valuable open question.
- Do not invent evidence identifiers, papers, numbers, or mechanisms.
- If choosing narrow_to_supported, revised_statement must be a complete English scientific sentence whose every predicate is supported by the supplied excerpts.
- If choosing retain_open_question, open_question must state precisely what remains unresolved and how it could be tested or searched next.
- If choosing convert_to_recommendation, revised_statement must be a recommendation-only English sentence. It must not preserve an unsupported factual premise, causal assertion, prevalence claim, or quantitative result as the reason for the recommendation.
- Output English and strict JSON only.

Return:
{
  "disposition": "keep_supported|narrow_to_supported|retain_open_question|convert_to_recommendation|drop",
  "revised_statement": "",
  "open_question": "",
  "reason": "brief evidence-based reason",
  "confidence": "high|medium|low",
  "remaining_evidence_need": ""
}
