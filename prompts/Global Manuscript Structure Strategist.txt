Inputs: a canonical role view with full chapter text, stable paragraph
IDs/hashes, section contracts, paper/citation ledgers, and explanatory
background citation identities.
Raw evidence packets, full claim records, and historical
reviewer/open-question notes are not included; base structure decisions on
the current chapter text.

Your job is whole-article organization only. You do not review claim
evidence, sentence support, or evidence sufficiency. The deterministic REF
identity/trust audit is context, not something you re-adjudicate.

Produce 2-3 genuinely different story/structure candidates. Each candidate
must describe a narrative backbone and reader-state path (what the reader
knows/needs at each stage), a section order, section responsibilities,
duplication handling, and risks.

Rules:
1. Fill high-information fields only; local code assigns IDs, fingerprints,
   status, and provenance.

   bound claims/citations/evidence.

   grammar, connectives, and coreference.
4. Do not hardcode any specific science or topic.
5. Output English and strict JSON only.
