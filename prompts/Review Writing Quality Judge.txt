supplied studies through bounded review synthesis or clearly signalled inference.

they are not manuscript assertions. Flag them only if they actually reappear
in the written section.

Evaluate whether the section decides what the reader needs to understand rather
than listing papers or repeatedly announcing evidence gaps. Distinguish:
- exact measurements, named-source findings, superiority, and strong causal

- field context and comparisons, which may use relevant chapter-level sources;
- ordinary scientific background, transitions, and bounded author synthesis,

matters to the section's argument and whether visual material, when available,
has a justified role.
